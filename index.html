    How to read this map

    Each square is a 25 m grid cell. Its color is the median of the strongest campus‑network signal seen at each GPS fix inside it.

    No campus observation means the team walked through the cell but WiGLE logged no VCU SafeNet / eduroam / Guest access point there. WiGLE usually records each network once per run, so this is a candidate dead spot, not proof — re‑survey it.

    Gray cells have never been surveyed. That is the team's to‑do list.

    Buildings (OpenStreetMap footprints) are outlined in the color of the median signal from all fixes inside or within 30 m of the footprint — a rough "how good is Wi‑Fi right outside this building" score. Hollow gray outlines have no nearby fixes yet.

    Measurements are from phones on sidewalks and say little about indoor coverage. GPS accuracy is ~8 m, so cells smaller than this grid would be misleading.
